Learn: Scattering

In 1911, Ernest Rutherford discovered that the atom is mostly empty space with a tiny, dense, positively charged nucleus at its center.

Alpha particles are positively charged. As they approach the nucleus, the Coulomb Force causes them to repel.

Observation: Higher energy particles (MeV) move faster and deflect less, while a higher Atomic Number (Z) increases the repulsive force.
